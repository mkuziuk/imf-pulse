## Signal 01 — Lemma 2.5 is narrower than the internal review says

The rendered draft first defines ordinary $W^{(k)}$ and the composite recursive operator $\mathcal W^{(k)}=W^{(k)}A^{(k-1)}$ as visibly different objects. Lemma 2.5 then prints **ordinary $W$** in

$$
(W^{(k)}W^{(k)\top})_{tt}\asymp (nh_k)^{-1}.
$$

The theory audit, review, and scaling note instead transcribe the lemma with calligraphic $\mathcal W$. That is a real contradiction, not a stylistic variant. The primary rendering controls: as written, the lemma concerns the single-pass linear smoother, not the full recursive component. It is also unproved and gives only an order, not equality, realized RMSE, or a sup-error result. The PDF text extractor flattens both glyphs, so this is a reviewed visual observation tied to the PDF hash and pages, not a machine-text inference. **Confidence:** very high about the printed glyph; low about unstated authorial intent. A missing calligraphic mark remains possible. [Evidence: IMF.pdf, pp. 5–6, §2.5.1 and Lemma 2.5](/sources#src-imf-draft); [conflicting audit, lines 5–19](/sources#src-theory-audit).

## Signal 02 — The first-stage jump has an exact finite-sample explanation

The recursive error does not need Lemma 2.5 to explain its shape. For the implemented linear wrap filter,

$$
e_k=W_k\prod_{j<k}(I-W_j)\varepsilon.
$$

Stage 1 is an unsuppressed low-pass output with DC gain one. Every later stage contains residual factors, removes DC, and behaves as a lower-energy band-pass detail. The exact bandwidth-scaled RMS is $0.02018$ at stage 1 and averages $0.00678$ over stages 2–9—a ratio of $2.98$. Five thousand Gaussian trials reproduce the pattern. **Confidence:** very high for this filter and covariance model. **Limitation:** the constants depend on the current kernel formula, windows, $n$, boundary, and white-noise assumption. [Exact rows 2–10](/sources#src-linear-operator-exact); [summary `/linear_exact`](/sources#src-diagnostics-summary).

## Signal 03 — Contamination is not the cause

Across 120 robust Gaussian-only trials, the scaled stage-one/tail ratio is $2.84$; with signed contamination it is $2.82$. Contamination raises absolute error but barely changes the structural contrast. **Confidence:** high for this synthetic setup, not universal. The robust method has no fixed global operator, uses a clean same-contrast target rather than the draft’s expected-noisy-loss target, and fixes $H=2\sigma$ across stages. [Robust Monte Carlo, rows 2–19](/sources#src-robust-monte-carlo); [summary `/robust_mc`](/sources#src-diagnostics-summary).

## Why this matters

Three questions must stay separate: whether a single-pass smoother follows a bandwidth rate, whether a recursive linear component is calibrated to its complete operator energy, and whether a nonlinear robust recursion is stable around a scientifically appropriate target. Merging them turns a precise numerical result into an unsupported theorem. The evidence supports the operator diagnosis strongly; it does not repair the draft or prove recursive robust theory.

## Unresolved question

Across the full recursion, how far does the clean same-contrast robust reference move from the expected-noisy-loss population target as $H/\sigma$, contamination, and stage vary—and do held-out bootstrap or Jacobian scales remain calibrated to that target?
